## Status

- Co-founder and coordinator of the [GeomScale](https://geomscale.github.io/) project, conducting R&D on high-dimensional geometric computing, statistics, and optimization. 

- Researcher at the [DSO lab](https://tto.ntua.gr/en/ntua-labs-en/school-rural-surveying-engineering-en/lab-dionysos-satellite-observatory-en/), NTUA, working on satellite trajectories, including methods and software for precise orbit determination (POD) and positioning.

- Software engineer in the [LibreOffice](https://www.libreoffice.org/) project, focusing on Base development, the suite’s database front end and database management component.

- Lecturer at the [Department of Informatics and Telecommunications](https://www.di.uoa.gr/en/studies/graduate/cs) of [NKUA](https://en.uoa.gr/), teaching MSc courses and supervising diploma theses.

- Scientific collaborator with the [OURAGAN team](https://team.inria.fr/ouragan/) at INRIA.

## Short Bio

Vissarion Fisikopoulos works on geometric and statistical computing and optimization, from algorithms with provable guarantees to their engineering into open-source software.

From 2016 to 2025 he was a member of the MySQL query optimizer team at Oracle, conducting research and development on query optimization and on geometric algorithms for spatial databases in [Boost.Geometry](https://www.boost.org/libs/geometry/), the geometry engine behind [MySQL](https://www.mysql.com/)'s spatial support.

Earlier, he was a postdoctoral researcher in the [Algorithms Research Group](https://algo.ulb.be) at [Université libre de Bruxelles (ULB)](https://www.ulb.be/en), working with Prof. [Samuel Fiorini](https://samuel.fiorini.web.ulb.be) on combinatorial optimization and polyhedral combinatorics. Before that, he was a visiting researcher at the National Institute for Mathematical Sciences in South Korea and a researcher at the University of Athens.

He holds a PhD in Computer Science from the [University of Athens](https://en.uoa.gr/), supervised by Prof. [Ioannis Emiris](https://cgi.di.uoa.gr/~emiris/index-eng.html), an MSc in Logic, Algorithms and Computation from the University of Athens, and a Diploma (5-year degree) in Computer Engineering & Computer Science from [CEID, University of Patras](https://www.ceid.upatras.gr/).

## Research Interests

- Optimization and geometry: convex and combinatorial optimization, polyhedral combinatorics, high-dimensional computational geometry.
- Foundations of data science: sampling, integration, volume computation, computational statistics.
- Mathematical software and algorithm engineering: open-source tools for numerical, geometric and statistical computing.
- GIS, spatial databases, satellite geodesy, precise orbit determination (POD), positioning.
- Applications in computational finance and systems biology.
